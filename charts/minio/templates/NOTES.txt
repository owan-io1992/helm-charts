Your MinIO has been deployed.

1. Get your MinIO credentials:
   Username: {{ .Values.minio.minio_root_user }}
   Password:
     export MINIO_ROOT_PASSWORD=$(kubectl get secret --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} {{ include "minio.fullname" . }} -o jsonpath="{.data.MINIO_ROOT_PASSWORD}" | base64 -d)
     echo "MinIO Password: $MINIO_ROOT_PASSWORD"

2. Access the MinIO Console:
   You can access the MinIO console by port-forwarding the console service.

   kubectl port-forward --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/{{ include "minio.fullname" . }}-console 9090:{{ .Values.console.service.port }}

   Then open your browser and go to http://127.0.0.1:9090

3. Access the MinIO S3 API:
   You can also access the MinIO S3 API directly.

   kubectl port-forward --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/{{ include "minio.fullname" . }}-s3api 9000:{{ .Values.minio.service.s3api.port }}

   The S3 endpoint will be available at http://127.0.0.1:9000
